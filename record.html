World Record

Working toward the most college degrees held by one person

World record

I am working toward the world record for the most college degrees held by one person. Between 2000 and 2026 I have earned 40 degrees from eight California and Mississippi colleges and universities: two bachelor's degrees, three master's degrees and 35 associate degrees, along with two general education certificates.

Most of the associate degrees came between 2014 and 2020, earned at five Sacramento-area community colleges while I was teaching mathematics full time.

In November 2021 I applied to Guinness World Records for a "Most College Degrees" title. In February 2022 Guinness declined the application, explaining that the category could not be standardized for record purposes, so no official title exists for it today. I plan to apply again in the hope that their policy changes, and I am reaching out to local media to share the project in the meantime.

Background

People who keep enrolling long after a first degree are sometimes called perpetual students. The phrase is often used as a mild insult, but many who earn it describe what they do as lifelong learning.

The pattern is old. William Cullen Bryant Kemp earned eleven degrees from Columbia University over more than six decades before his death in 1928, and Paul Cousins spent 39 years on a Columbia PhD, finally finishing it in 1966.

The modern record holders mostly collect graduate degrees. Michael Nicholson, an American who has worked as a part-time parking attendant, is listed with 30 degrees, more than twenty of them master's degrees. Robert W. McGee, an accounting professor at Fayetteville State University, holds 23, including 13 doctorates. In India, the civil servant Shrikant Jichkar earned 20 degrees before his death in 2004. Stephanie Attwater of Canada also holds 20 and has the record for the most degrees earned by a woman.

Others took longer routes through a single school. Benjamin Bolger moved from Muskegon Community College to the University of Michigan and then Harvard, ending with sixteen degrees including a doctorate in design. In Italy, Guinness World Records recognized Luciano Baietti in 2002 as the living person with the most degrees, then 15. Kaija Hammar has studied at Finland's University of Jyväskylä since 1974, building up course credit equal to more than eleven degrees.
