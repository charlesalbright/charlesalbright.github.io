Research

Papers, projects and talks

Work

  • Zero to Hero: The Case for 00 = 1 Mathematics Paper in preparation

    Brings together several earlier drafts and a talk into one argument that 00 should be defined as 1.

  • Toward a General Theory of Closed Forms Mathematics Work in progress

    Surveys the literature on closed-form functions and numbers and argues that there is no absolute sense in which a function or number is closed.

  • On Grim's Argument Against a Set of All Truths Philosophy Paper in preparation

    Examines Patrick Grim's Cantorian argument that there is no set of all truths.

  • Talking About How We Talk About Slurs in Philosophic Research Philosophy UC Davis, 2021 · revision in progress

    On the conventions philosophers use when mentioning slurs in their own research writing.

  • Mathematical Methodology: How Proofs Are Written, Checked and Trusted Foundations Research theme

    How research mathematicians actually establish results: proofs that leave key steps unexplained, results proved with AI assistance that the authors themselves do not fully understand, and errors in published proofs that go uncaught for years or are never caught.

  • Mathematical Proof Analyzer Computation Project idea

    A program that reads proofs written in ordinary mathematical prose and determines which formal axiomatic systems could support a formal version of each proof, as a way to see which foundations working mathematicians actually rely on.
