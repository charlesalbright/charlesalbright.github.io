About

I am a tenured Professor of Mathematics at Sierra College in Rocklin, California, where I have taught full time since 2015. I have taught mathematics in the Sacramento region since 2008, at Sacramento State, Cosumnes River College and Sierra College, and served as a teaching assistant in philosophy at UC Davis.

My training spans mathematics, philosophy and computational engineering, and my research sits where those fields meet: the foundations of mathematics, logic, and the philosophy of language.
